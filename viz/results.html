Explainable bank AI can leak real customers' bank histories

Shapelet models explain a money-laundering alert with a short pattern. That pattern is often copied from a real customer. We tested how bad that is.

What we found

86%of the best shapelet model's patterns point to exactly one real customer.About 830 people per model, with their weekly amounts.
96%of the attacker's guesses are right even when their copy of the data is messy.Every amount off by 5%.

Bottom line

  • Sharing a shapelet model can share pieces of real customers' histories, including who was flagged.
  • The usual privacy test won't catch it.
  • Hiding the amounts doesn't fix it. Learned shapelets do.

Limits: both datasets are synthetic, and the attacker needs most of a person's transactions.
