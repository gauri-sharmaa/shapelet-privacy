Explainable bank AI can leak real customers' bank histories

Shapelet models explain a money-laundering alert with a short pattern. That pattern is often copied from a real customer. We tested how bad that is.

What we found

Bottom line

  • Sharing a shapelet model can share pieces of real customers' histories, including who was flagged.
  • It happens with real customers too, and in big banks.
  • The usual privacy test won't catch it. A per-person test does.
  • Hiding the amounts doesn't fix it. Learned shapelets do.

Limits: the attacker needs most of a person's transactions; two of the three datasets are synthetic.
